GLITCH - ESCAPE THE SIMULATION

GAME DESCRIPTION

You are trapped inside a digital simulation controlled by a mysterious system called NEXUS. Choose to play as a Hacker or a Detective, uncover hidden secrets, solve puzzles, and break through the system's security. Every challenge brings you closer to freedom.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Click NEW GAME to begin.
  2. Choose HACKER or DETECTIVE.
  3. Select one of the two storylines.
  4. Read the clues and solve the puzzles.
  5. Select the correct symbols and click CONFIRM to complete the symbol challenges.
  6. Connect START to EXIT in the network maze before time runs out.
  7. Watch your suspicion level. Too many mistakes may alert NEXUS.
  8. Complete the final challenge to escape the simulation!
